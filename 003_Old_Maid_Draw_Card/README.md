# Old Maid &lt;card-t> UI drag &amp; drop or Click &amp; drop game


### Right most player's card can be moved to your piles(bottom piles)

## Game process:

1. Drag or click a card from right most player

2. Drag to bottom pile area, or click bottom pile area

3. Wait for computer players playing

> [!NOTE]
> Red card is Joker
