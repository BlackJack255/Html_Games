Four Jacks Card Game

### Variations from original Game(See [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polignac_(card_game)))
### loser in a trick may got Jack, with cause point loss


## Game Process: 

This is a trick-taking game

card rank comparison: A > K > Q > J > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7

1. follow the lead suit if possible, others free to discard any card.

2. The trick is won by the player played highest rank of the lead suit.

3. Winner do next trick lead.

4. If all four players follow lead suit, player played lowest rank lost the trick.

5. If only one player discard, discard player lost the trick.

6. If more than one players discard, lowest rank discard player lost the trick, suit doesn't matter.

7. IF more than one players discard and same lowest rank, last ordered player from lead position lost. (eg North lead SA, the from East South West played: D10 HJ C10, both East and West discard 10 lowest, but West played later than East, so West lost the trick )

8. Loser need take all 4 cards in a trick.

9. After 8 tricks end, each player count cards taken from his/her lost tricks.

10. Each Jack taken loses 1 point.

11. Players lost most points losses.
